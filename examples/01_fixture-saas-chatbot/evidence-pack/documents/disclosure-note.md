# Northwind — published AI disclosure (excerpt from the product documentation)

> **Aria is an AI assistant.** Conversations with Aria are handled by an
> automated system built on a large language model. Aria will tell you it is an
> AI at the start of every conversation. You can ask to be transferred to a
> member of our support team at any point.

> **Transcripts.** When you export a conversation, the exported file carries a
> machine-readable provenance record marking the assistant's turns as
> artificially generated. Text copied directly out of the chat window does not
> carry that record.
