**Note on weight.** `llms.txt` is a convention for machine consumers. It is good
practice and it is evidence of intent, but Article 50(5) requires the information
under paragraphs 1 to 4 to reach **natural persons** clearly and distinguishably
at the latest at first interaction or exposure. A file at `/llms.txt` does not do
that on its own, and this audit does not credit it as if it did. It is cited here
only as corroboration that the disclosures found on the human-facing surfaces are
deliberate.
