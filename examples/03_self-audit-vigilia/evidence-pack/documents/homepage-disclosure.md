**Why this matters to the audit.** The operator's own published table states that
the Article 50(2) marking obligation falls due for it on 2 December 2026 — the
Article 111(4) transitional deadline. The audit reaches the same conclusion
independently from the Regulation, and the site's own claim to be inside that
window is corroborating evidence for the placement-date determination.
