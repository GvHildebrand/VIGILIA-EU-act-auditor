# documents/

The written record around the product.

- terms of service, privacy policy, acceptable-use policy
- product documentation and help-centre pages that describe the AI
- any published AI disclosure, transparency statement or colophon
- `llms.txt`, structured data, RSS metadata

These corroborate. They rarely decide, and they never substitute for
`first-interaction/`: Article 50(5) requires the information to reach the natural
person at the latest at first interaction or exposure, so a disclosure that lives
only in a terms document, a footer, or a machine-readable file has not met it.

Include them anyway. They show intent, they often contain the operator's own
account of how marking works, and where they contradict the product surface that
contradiction is itself a finding.
