# first-interaction/

What a natural person meets **first**, before they have done anything.

Article 50(5) requires the information under paragraphs 1 to 4 to be provided
"in a clear and distinguishable manner at the latest at the time of the first
interaction or exposure". This folder is how that gets assessed, so capture the
moment of first contact — not the best disclosure you have somewhere on the site.

Put in whichever apply:

- the opening message or screen of a chat interface (text or captured HTML)
- landing page copy above the fold, in rendered order
- the first thing a voice agent says
- the byline area of a published page, as a reader meets it
- screenshots, where markup will not carry the point

Include the **markup** where you can. Whether a disclosure is real text or a
styled image decides the accessibility limb of 50(5), and it cannot be told from
a screenshot.

Say plainly what is *not* there. "No statement at this surface that the output is
AI-generated" is evidence, and it is often the finding.
